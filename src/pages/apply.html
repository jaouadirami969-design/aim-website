Good to know

I'm 13. Can I fill in the form myself?

Please ask a parent or guardian to do it for you (and tick "Parent or guardian"). Quebec's privacy law (Law 25) asks for a parent's consent before we collect personal information from anyone 13 or under.

What happens with my information?

We use it to send you the info pack and to answer you — and to send occasional news only if you ticked the second box. We never sell or share it. You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to {{ site.email }}. See our privacy and consent page.

Can a school or a counselor register several students?

Yes. Tick "Counselor or teacher", get the info pack, then reply to it with roughly how many students — we'll set up a group conversation.

I'd rather talk to someone.

Write to {{ site.email }} and suggest a time — we're happy to call parents back.

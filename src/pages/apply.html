Before you send

I'm 13. Can I fill in the form myself?

Please ask a parent or guardian to send it for you (and tick "Parent of a high school student"). Quebec's privacy law (Law 25) asks for a parent's consent before we collect personal information from anyone 13 or under.

What happens with my information?

We use it to answer you and to send news about the program — nothing else. We never sell or share it. You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to {{ site.email }}. See our privacy and consent page.

Can a school or a counselor register several students?

Yes. Choose "College / University counselor" or "Adviser", tell us roughly how many students, and we'll set up a group conversation.

I'd rather talk to someone.

Write to {{ site.email }} and suggest a time — we're happy to call parents back.

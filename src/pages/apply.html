Questions families ask

Good to know before you send

What happens after I submit?

You receive a copy of your answers by email right away (the parent or guardian too). Ilyas or Rami reads every application personally and we email our decision within {{ site.apply.decisionDays }} business days. Accepted students then receive a link to reserve their seat — registration opens in December 2026, and nothing is due before that.

Is it first come, first served?

Seats are limited — 32 students per campus session — so earlier applications have more choice of session and format. We read every application, though: we're looking for curiosity and the right age range, not a finance background or top grades.

I'm 13. Can I fill in the application myself?

You can start it, but under Quebec's privacy law (Law 25) we need a parent or guardian's consent before we collect information from anyone under 14. The simplest is to fill it in together — or to let your parent do it and tick "I'm a parent or guardian". Either way, the parent receives a one-click confirmation email.

Can I stop and finish later?

Yes. Your answers are saved on this device as you type. Come back to this page and press Continue where I left off. If more than two hours have passed we'll ask you to confirm your email again — a new code, ten seconds.

Which class should we choose?

In summer 2027, everyone starts with the Beginner class — it assumes nothing and goes far. Intermediate and Advanced open in the following summers for students who completed the previous level with AIM. The program page describes all three.

Campus or online?

Same instructor, same lessons, same certificate. The McGill campus class (9 am – 3 pm) is the full-day experience with the afternoon Investment Lab in person. Live online (1 – 3 pm) works from anywhere and keeps the mornings free. Not sure? Pick No preference and we'll suggest the best fit.

What happens with our information?

It's used only to review the application and to organize the program. It's stored on access-controlled servers in Canada and never sold or shared with advertisers. You can ask us to correct or delete it at any time at {{ site.email }}. See our privacy and consent page.

I'd rather talk to someone first.

Write to {{ site.email }} and suggest a time — we're happy to call parents back, in English or in French. Or get the info pack first.

d Built by a student, for students

Why we started AIM

AIM began with a question from a 16-year-old: why is it so hard to learn how money and the stock market really work before you turn 18? Rami Jaouadi took a two-week finance camp in Montreal, loved it, and teamed up with his mentor Ilyas Zakiat — a CEO who teaches finance at McGill — to build something better: a program with real depth, a real campus, and a real capstone.

The free lessons on this site are Rami's part of the deal — short videos that explain finance the way he wishes someone had explained it to him. The summer program is Ilyas's: eight lessons that take you from your first dollar to your first portfolio strategy.

Why we started AIM

AIM began with a question from a 16-year-old: why is it so hard to learn how money and the stock market really work before you turn 18? Rami Jaouadi took a two-week finance class taught by Ilyas Zakiat and loved it. He wanted more students to learn this before they turn 18. So he teamed up with Ilyas — a CEO who teaches finance at McGill — and they built AIM together.

The free lessons on this site are Rami's part of the deal — short guides that explain finance the way he wishes someone had explained it to him. The summer program is Ilyas's: eight lessons that take you from your first dollar to your first portfolio strategy.

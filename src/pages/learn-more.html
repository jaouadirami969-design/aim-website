Good to know

I didn't receive the code.

Give it a minute, then look in Spam, Junk or Promotions. School and work mailboxes sometimes hold new senders for a few minutes. You can ask for a new code after 60 seconds — the previous one stops working. Still nothing? Write to {{ site.email }} and we'll send the info pack by hand.

What do you do with my email address?

We send you the info pack and we answer your questions. That's it. We only send occasional news if you ticked the second box, and every email has an unsubscribe link. We never sell or share addresses. Details on our privacy and consent page.

I'm 13. Can I ask for the info pack myself?

Please ask a parent or guardian to do it for you. Quebec's privacy law (Law 25) asks for a parent's consent before we collect personal information from anyone 13 or under — and every registration is confirmed by a parent anyway.

Is this the application?

No — this just sends you the information. Applying takes about 10 minutes on the Apply page, with no transcripts or recommendation letters. Registration for Summer 2027 opens in December 2026.

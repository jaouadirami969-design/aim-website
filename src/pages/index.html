Why AIM?

Three things you won't find together in any other finance program for teens in Montreal.

  • A CEO as your teacher

    Ilyas Zakiat is the CEO and founder of BIASafe AI, a former portfolio manager, and a finance lecturer at McGill. You learn how investing is actually done — from someone who does it.

  • A real university campus

    Spend two weeks on the McGill University campus in downtown Montreal, 8 am to 2 pm, Monday to Friday — or follow the same class live online from anywhere.

  • A million-dollar capstone

    You'll run a simulated $1,000,000 portfolio with real market data, choose one strategy, and defend it on pitch day — then leave with a certificate of completion.

Privacy & consent

Your information, in plain English.

We collect as little as possible, we use it only to run the program, and we never sell it. This page explains exactly what that means — including what Quebec's privacy law (Law 25) requires for students 13 and under.

What we collect

When you ask for the info pack ("Learn more"), we collect what you type: your email address, your first name if you give it, whether you are a student, a parent or a counselor, what you're interested in, and the two consent boxes (with the date and the exact wording you agreed to). To protect the form from abuse we also keep a one-way, hashed version of your network address for a few weeks, the page you came from, and the type of browser you used. The 6-digit code we email you is stored hashed, expires after 10 minutes and is never shown to us.

When you apply ("Apply now"), we collect what the six steps ask: the email address you verify with a code, who is filling in the form, the student's name, date of birth and address, a parent or guardian's name, email and phone number, the program, class, format and session you'd like, the school and level, the optional motivation text, and the three consent boxes (with the date and the exact wording). We also keep the same abuse-protection details as above (hashed network address, page, browser). While you fill in the form, your answers are saved in your own browser and, once your email is verified, on our server, so you can finish later; a reference number (AIM-27-…) identifies the application in every email. When the student is under 14, the parent or guardian receives a one-click confirmation link, as Law 25 requires.

When a student registers for a session, we also collect payment confirmation (payments are processed by a secure payment provider; we never see or store card numbers).

The three free lessons and this website can be used without creating an account and without giving us any personal information.

Why we collect it

  • To answer your questions and tell you about sessions, dates and registration.
  • To run the program: attendance, class materials, the student portal, the certificate.
  • To contact a parent or guardian quickly if needed during a session.
  • To send occasional news about AIM — only if you ticked the second box, and you can unsubscribe in one click, at any time.

Every info-pack request and every application also sends an alert to our own mailbox, so a real person can answer you within two business days.

Students 13 and under — a parent's consent

Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (updated by Law 25) says that personal information about a minor under 14 cannot be collected without the consent of a parent or guardian, unless it is clearly for the minor's benefit. So:

  • If you are 13 or younger, please ask a parent or guardian to fill in the form and to register you.
  • Every registration, at any age, is confirmed by a parent or guardian.

Who sees it

Only the AIM team (Rami Jaouadi and Ilyas Zakiat) and the service providers we need to run the website, email and payments. We never sell, rent or trade personal information, and we never share it with advertisers.

Our providers today: Supabase stores the info-pack requests and the applications in a database located in Canada (Montréal region); Brevo sends our emails (servers in the European Union); Google Workspace hosts our mailbox; GitHub Pages serves this website. Each one is bound by a data-processing agreement and only processes the data we send it.

How long we keep it

Info-pack requests are kept for up to 24 months after our last exchange; verification codes are deleted after 30 days. Applications that are started but never sent are deleted after 60 days. Applications that are not accepted are deleted 12 months after the decision. Student records are kept for the time needed to issue the certificate and to keep an attendance record, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Write to {{ site.email }} — we answer within 30 days, usually much faster. The person responsible for the protection of personal information at AIM is Rami Jaouadi, reachable at the same address.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS. Form submissions are sent over an encrypted connection and stored on access-controlled services — the database is not readable from the website itself, only by our server-side functions. Student accounts will use individual passwords, and parents will have their own access.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we embed a video, it is loaded from YouTube's privacy-enhanced player only when you press play.

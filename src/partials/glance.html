Class description

Learn how money really works, what moves the stock market and how real investors choose what to buy — then put it into practice by managing a simulated $1,000,000 portfolio. In two weeks and eight lessons, guided by a CEO who teaches finance at McGill, you'll go from curious to confident: value investing, growth investing, reading financial statements and building a diversified portfolio.
